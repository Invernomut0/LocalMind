# Code of Conduct

LocalMind follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the LocalMind community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent; verify before escalating.
- Critique ideas, not people.
- Accept feedback gracefully.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report issues to the maintainers via a private GitHub issue or by email (address published at v1.0). Reports will be reviewed within a reasonable timeframe and handled confidentially. Consequences range from a private warning to a permanent ban depending on severity.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all LocalMind community spaces — GitHub repository, Discussions, Discord, social channels — and to public events where someone represents the project.
